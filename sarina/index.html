The Woman on 1 November

The changes I have made are that my body has become much stronger and healthier. I passed my Theory Test, and now I am working on my practical test. It feels amazing to know that in these past 4 months, I have achieved the things I promised myself I would do. 🤗

The things I have let go of, like sleeping late and waking up late, helped me so much. Waking up earlier means I can get everything done, and I feel much better mentally and physically. 🤭

I am really proud of myself for passing my Theory Test because I worked so hard for it. I had to take the test three times before I passed. I am also proud that I managed to find a job so I can have my own money. 🫣

I have shown my mum and dad how much I have improved in these 4 months, even through the small progress I made. The most important thing is that I have proved to myself that if I truly want something, I can achieve it. 😎

I also want Mark to know that the person he is spending his time with is someone who is worth it. 🙂
